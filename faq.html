01

Pricing & Budgeting

Costs vary depending on project scale, deliverables, and production value. Simple single-camera interviews, event coverage or social media clips typically start between $1,000 and $3,500, while multi-day brand films, commercial shoots, or extensive editing generally range from $5,000 to $25,000+. Every project receives a custom line-item quote based on your specific requirements.

Primary cost drivers include:

  • Days & Crew SizeNumber of shoot days, camera operators, sound engineers, gaffers, and directors.
  • Talent & LocationsPaid actors, voiceover artists, studio rentals, and location permits.
  • Post-ProductionComplexity of motion graphics, visual effects, custom sound design, and colour grading.
  • DeliverablesThe total number of cut-downs and aspect ratios requested (e.g., 16:9 for YouTube vs. 9:16 for TikTok).
02

Process & Timelines

Standard corporate or promotional projects take 3 to 8 weeks from initial kickoff to final delivery:

  1. Pre-Production (1–2 weeks)Strategy, scriptwriting, storyboarding, and scheduling.
  2. Production (1–5 days)Filming and audio capture.
  3. Post-Production (2–3 weeks)Editing, colour grading, sound mixing, and client revision rounds.

Projects move through three clear phases:

  1. Pre-ProductionAlign on goals, write scripts, plan camera and shot technique, cast talent, and lock in the shoot schedule.
  2. ProductionArrive on location with cinema-grade cameras, audio, and lighting gear to capture all required footage.
  3. Post-ProductionWe edit the visual cut, add music, sound effects, motion graphics, and colour grade, delivering drafts through an online platform for your team's feedback.
03

Creative & Client Involvement

No. If you have a fully formed concept, we will bring it to life. If you only have a high-level business objective, we will handle concept development, messaging structure, and scriptwriting from scratch.

Your heaviest involvement happens during pre-production to approve creative direction, scripts, and schedules. During post-production, we ask for feedback at specific milestone cuts (First Cut and Fine Cut). On filming days, a team member is welcome to accompany the crew on set, but we manage all logistics.

Standard packages include two rounds of revisions (First Cut for structure/pacing and Fine Cut for minor tweaks). Revisions are submitted via an interactive online tool where you can pin comments directly to exact timestamps on the video frame.

04

Licensing & Technical Deliverables

Final files are exported in high-bitrate 4K or 1080p .mp4 format, optimised for web, television, and social media. We provide cut-downs built for specific channels:

  • 16:9 (Horizontal)Websites, YouTube, LinkedIn, TV.
  • 9:16 (Vertical)Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
  • 1:1 (Square)Instagram Feed and paid social ad placements.

Upon final payment, your company receives full licensing rights to host, distribute, and broadcast the final edited video indefinitely. Raw camera footage (unedited clips) remains the property of LSC Creative, but can be purchased as an add-on alongside a hard drive delivery fee.
